Through Our Eyes

Tap. Discover. Learn.

Step into 3D healthcare environments. Tap the glowing hotspots to find sensory issues and see what good looks like. Then build your own sensory plan.

How it works

  • 1Explore - Each room has glowing hotspots. Tap them to reveal the sensory issue.
  • 2Compare - In the waiting room, toggle between the current room and a sensory-friendly alternative.
  • 3Learn - Each room connects to a SOFT(TM) Framework principle.
  • 4Plan - Finish with a How to Work With Me template to fill in and print.
Disclaimer: This interactive training is for information and awareness purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice and should not replace the guidance of a qualified healthcare professional. Always consult an appropriate professional for individual needs.
