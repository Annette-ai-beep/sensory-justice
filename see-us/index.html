The See Us Campaign

We are not asking for better waiting rooms.
We are demanding to be seen.

There is no mandatory sensory awareness training in the NHS. Staff see neurodivergent patients every day without the knowledge or tools to care for them. This campaign exists to change that.

The Demand

Mandatory sensory awareness training for all NHS patient-facing staff.

What we mean by "real training"

  • · Understanding sensory processing differences
  • · Recognising sensory distress in clinical settings
  • · Practical environmental adaptations
  • · Communication strategies for neurodivergent patients
  • · Lived experience input in training design

What we do not accept

  • · A leaflet in a staff handbook
  • · A 20-minute e-learning module nobody finishes
  • · “Reasonable adjustments” left to individual staff goodwill
  • · Tick-box diversity training with no sensory content
  • · Another review, another report, another delay

The Problem

The system is not malicious. It is untrained.

15-20%

of the UK population is neurodivergent. Every NHS staff member sees us daily.

0

mandatory hours of sensory awareness training required for NHS patient-facing staff.

100%

of neurodivergent patients deserve care that accounts for how their nervous system works.

The NHS Constitution promises dignity and respect. But dignity and respect mean nothing if the environment itself causes harm and no one on staff knows why.

Write to Your MP

Five minutes. One email. Real change.

MPs respond to numbers. Copy the template below, find your MP, add your name, and send. That is it.

Template Letter

Dear [Your MP name],

I am writing to ask you to support mandatory sensory awareness training for all NHS patient-facing staff.

Currently, there is no requirement for NHS staff to receive training in sensory processing differences, despite an estimated 15-20% of the UK population being neurodivergent. This means NHS staff are interacting with neurodivergent patients every day without the knowledge or tools to provide appropriate care.

I am asking you to:

1. Support the call for mandatory sensory awareness training in the NHS
2. Raise this issue with the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care
3. Ask what steps the Department of Health is taking to address sensory barriers in NHS care

[Optional: Add one or two sentences about your own experience here]

Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to your response.

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]
[Your postcode]
