About me

I'm a neurodivergent healthcare designer and patient advocate. After my own cancer treatment, I saw how sensory-unsafe environments fail neurodivergent patients — so I started building what was missing: training, resources and campaigns that make healthcare actually see us. Everything here is built from lived experience, not assumptions.

Level 2 Understanding Autism ATC Member CMA Member Neurodivergent patient & carer Founder, Burnt Orchid Organics
